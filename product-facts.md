# DropBrief — Product Facts & Verification

- **Product Name**: DropBrief
- **Live URL**: https://www.usedropbrief.xyz/#
- **Product Category**: Micro-SaaS client asset & brief collection portal for freelancers, boutique creative agencies, web designers, brand identity designers, and accountants.
- **Problem Statement**:
  - Web designers and creative freelancers waste hours chasing clients for vector logos, copy, high-res photos, and login credentials across WhatsApp, email attachments, and broken Google Drive permissions ("You need access").
  - Alternative tools like Content Snare are expensive ($29–$59/month), complex, and require accounts. WeTransfer links expire in 7 days. Email compresses files.
- **Core Value Proposition**:
  - **Zero-friction client portal**: Client clicks a single unique link (`usedropbrief.xyz/?p=brand-identity`) and uploads immediately. No registration, no login, no passwords, works on any smartphone or desktop browser.
  - **Uncompressed High-Res Assets**: Supports SVG, AI, EPS, PDF, PNG, JPG, ZIP up to 50MB per file directly stored in secure EU cloud storage.
  - **Real-Time Live Progress**: 0% to 100% checklist progress bar with instant status updates and confetti celebrations.
  - **Freelancer Toolkit**: Pre-built templates (Web Design, Brand Identity, Social Media, Accounting), 1-click friendly email reminders via Resend API, 1-click ZIP export, and automated GDPR 30-day file purging.
  - **Free & Cookie-Free**: 100% cookie-free, no ad trackers, 256-bit SSL, Supabase EU Ireland infrastructure.
