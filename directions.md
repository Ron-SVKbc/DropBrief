# DropBrief — 6 Candidates & 3 Chosen Directions

## Spec Summary
- **Target Audience**: Freelance web designers, brand identity designers, creative agencies, accountants.
- **Goal**: Convert visiting freelancers into users by letting them test the client portal instantly and start creating free project checklists.
- **Output Formats**: Single-file HTML prototypes (responsive, interactive, WCAG accessible, zero console errors).

---

## The 6 Direction Candidates

### Candidate 1 (Best Designer 1: Dieter Rams / Otl Aicher — Swiss Precision Studio)
- **Logic & Source**: Ulm School & Braun functional minimalism. Information density with crystal-clear hierarchy.
- **Borrowed Rules**: 1px crisp separation grid, asymmetric split-screen hero, deep contrast `#0F172A` on `#FFFFFF`, zero extraneous ornaments.
- **Hero Material**: Live interactive Dual-Column split: left side contains value proposition & 1-click test link; right side contains a live interactive miniature client checklist with real drag-and-drop file upload simulation.
- **Skeleton**: Split-screen hero, 2-column contrast table ("Google Drive Chaos vs DropBrief Flow"), step-by-step checklist preview, footer.
- **Bold Move**: No decorative illustrations; the interactive UI simulation itself IS the hero graphic.
- **Form Origin**: Web designers care about exact file types and clean grids; the page behaves like an exact design spec sheet.

### Candidate 2 (Best Designer 2: Richard Turley / Experimental Jetset — Editorial Impact Broadside)
- **Logic & Source**: Bloomberg Businessweek editorial brutality meets Swiss modernist poster art.
- **Borrowed Rules**: Giant display type (tight letter-spacing), extreme typographic contrast (96px bold title over 15px sharp body), heavy rule dividers, cobalt blue and signal orange accents.
- **Hero Material**: Bold typographic broadside statement: "YOUR CLIENT WILL NEVER ASK FOR A GOOGLE DRIVE PERMISSION AGAIN."
- **Skeleton**: Full-width high-impact statement banner, physical dossier-style feature cards, side-by-side diagnostic table.
- **Bold Move**: Unapologetic, headline-first visual rhythm with oversized typography that challenges SaaS clichés.
- **Form Origin**: Freelancers are frustrated by polite emails chasing clients for weeks; the blunt editorial headline speaks their exact language.

### Candidate 3 (Real Reference 1: Linear / Raycast — Midnight Pro Bento HUD)
- **Logic & Source**: Modern developer & digital designer aesthetic (Linear.app, Cursor, Raycast).
- **Borrowed Rules**: Obsidian dark mode `#0A0B10`, glowing subtle rim lighting, frosted glass bento cards (`backdrop-filter: blur`), glowing status pills, neon accents (`#3B82F6` and `#10B981`).
- **Hero Material**: Glowing centered product HUD with a live perspective switcher: toggle between "What Your Client Sees" (zero login, drag-and-drop) and "What You See" (real-time progress, 1-click ZIP).
- **Skeleton**: Central illuminated hero console, 4-tile Bento grid highlighting key features, interactive sandbox trigger, dark telemetry footer.
- **Bold Move**: Immersive dark atmosphere that feels like a precision high-performance instrument.
- **Form Origin**: Modern designers build in dark mode tools; seeing their client workflow packaged as a sleek Pro tool builds instant authority.

### Candidate 4 (Real Reference 2: Pitch / Stripe — Warm Kinetic Studio)
- **Logic & Source**: Stripe Press and Pitch.com presentation design.
- **Borrowed Rules**: Warm cream paper background `#FBF9F5`, warm terracotta `#D9532F` and deep navy `#1A2A44`, generous white space, tactile cards.
- **Hero Material**: Floating stacked brief cards (Branding, Web Design, Q4 Audit) showing live progress rings and client upload badges.
- **Skeleton**: Floating card stack hero, story-driven scroll, warm human agency aesthetic.
- **Bold Move**: Soft, tactile physical stationery feeling in a digital SaaS.
- **Form Origin**: High-end brand designers work with physical tactile materials; the warm aesthetic echoes premium stationery.

### Candidate 5 (Style Roulette: Web #02 Neo-Brutalist Feed)
- **Logic & Source**: High-contrast neo-brutalist cards with 3px solid black borders, hard offset drop shadows (`4px 4px 0 #000`), playful stickers.
- **Form Origin**: Indie hacker aesthetic with raw energy.

### Candidate 6 (Style Roulette: East #01 Asymmetric Calligraphic Minimal)
- **Logic & Source**: Japanese publication grid, vertical typographic accents, quiet generous negative space.
- **Form Origin**: Focus on absolute stillness and reduction of all clutter.

---

## Selection of the Top 3 Directions

We choose Candidates 1, 3, and 4/2 (re-synthesized into **Editorial Paper & Ink**):
1. **Direction 1: "Studio Precision" (Swiss Functionalist Light)**
   - Archetype: Clean, rational, high-clarity Light SaaS.
   - Core mechanic: Interactive split-screen with real-time file upload simulation.
2. **Direction 2: "Midnight Pro" (Linear Dark Bento HUD)**
   - Archetype: Sleek, obsidian dark mode with glowing bento cards & dual perspective toggle.
   - Core mechanic: Client vs Freelancer perspective switcher.
3. **Direction 3: "Editorial Paper & Ink" (Warm Agency Broadsheet)**
   - Archetype: Warm cream/terracotta editorial with serif headlines & authentic brief dossier.
   - Core mechanic: Tactile interactive checklist dossier with physical file chips.
