# DropBrief — Brand Asset Specification

## 1. Identity & Naming
- **Brand**: DropBrief
- **Domain**: `usedropbrief.xyz`
- **Tagline**: The zero-friction client brief & asset collection portal for freelancers & designers.
- **Tone of Voice**: Professional, friction-free, honest, empowering, developer/designer-friendly.

## 2. Core Visual Assets
- **Logo Icon**: `FolderSync` geometric folder with sync loop.
- **Brand Colors**:
  - Primary Brand Blue: `#2563EB` (Cobalt Royal Blue)
  - Deep Navy Slate: `#0F172A`
  - Emerald Success: `#10B981`
  - Warning Amber: `#F59E0B`
  - Surface Light: `#F6F7FB` / `#FFFFFF`
  - Surface Dark (Pro): `#0A0B10` / `#13151F`
  - Surface Paper (Warm Editorial): `#FBF9F5` / `#F0EAE1`

## 3. Typography
- **UI & Display**: Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Geist, Newsreader (warm editorial).
- **Code & Meta**: JetBrains Mono, Geist Mono.

## 4. Key Selling Arguments & Copy Points
- "Never chase a client for files again."
- "Zero logins, zero passwords, zero friction for your client."
- "No 'You need access' Google Drive errors. No expired WeTransfer links."
- "Real-time checklist with uncompressed file delivery up to 50MB."
- "100% Free forever & GDPR compliant (EU Ireland)."
